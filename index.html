About me

I meet people in their darkest places and help them discover their best self

Since 2004 I have worked alongside women in Central Asia, among them Uzbek, Uyghur and Kyrgyz women. Many had survived bride kidnapping, childhood sexual abuse or rape. For two decades God has used me to bring healing to those who are wounded.

Serving these women taught me that faith is lived out in what we do for one another. That is the heart of Wounded Healer.

I help my clients build a solid sense of self so they can stay grounded while facing life's challenges. Whether you are confronting your fears, mourning a loss, finding your voice or healing from trauma, I believe God is present and actively working on your behalf.

Vision

My vision

To bring healing and hope to people living with the trauma of sexual abuse and other mental health struggles, through Christian counselling and coaching, and to restore the dignity and worth of each person with the love of Christ.

Beliefs

What I believe

Every person has intrinsic significance. Every person's story matters and is worth hearing. Empathy and compassion come first, and you are always in charge of your own pace.
